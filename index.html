Event Transcripts

Missed a talk or want to revisit a specific message? Explore full transcripts of my past public speaking engagements below.

"Thank you all for being here today. When I first started sharing my story, I was terrified. I thought vulnerability was a liability. But what I've learned is that our darkest moments, when brought into the light, lose their power over us. We reclaim our narrative not by hiding, but by speaking out. Domestic violence thrives in silence. Mental health stigma thrives in shadows. By standing here today, sharing my truth, I hope to give you the permission to share yours. It isn't easy, and healing certainly isn't linear, but your voice is the most powerful tool you have to enact change."

"Good morning. Today I want to talk about ownership. Who owns your story? For a long time, I let my trauma write the chapters of my life. I let my BRCA diagnosis dictate my future with fear. The turning point came when I realized I was holding the pen all along. Taking preventative action wasn't giving up; it was taking control. It requires a profound amount of courage to look at the broken pieces of your life, or a terrifying medical reality, and decide you are going to build something beautiful and proactive out of them."

"Leaving is often framed as the end of the story, but for survivors, leaving is just page one of a brand new, very intimidating book. The isolation that comes after can sometimes feel as overwhelming as the abuse itself. That is why community is so vital. We must educate our neighborhoods, our workplaces, and our families not just on how to spot the signs of domestic violence, but on how to actively support survivors as they rebuild their lives from the ground up."
